Pancake Pile - Privacy policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026. Pancake Pile is a game by GPS Agents for Android.

The short version

  • You do not need an account. There is no sign-in and no sign-up.
  • The game itself does not collect any personal data from you. To show the banner ad, Google's ad service receives some device data; see Ads below.
  • Your best height, coins, unlocked items and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The game shows one small banner ad, which is non-personalised.

Who this game is for

Pancake Pile is made for all ages, including children. It has no chat, no way to talk to other players, no in-app purchases and no links that take you out of the game, apart from the banner ad, which opens Google Play or a web page if tapped.

What the game itself stores

The game keeps a small save file in the app's own storage on your device: your best height, your coins, the pancakes and themes you have unlocked and picked, and your settings (sound and vibration). This save never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

The game does not use analytics, does not track you, and has no servers of its own.

Ads

Pancake Pile shows one banner ad from Google AdMob on the menu, shop, settings and game-over screens, never while you are playing. Every ad request is set to be non-personalised, child-directed, for users under the age of consent, and limited to ads rated for general audiences (G). The game does not use the Android advertising ID.

To show an ad and to prevent fraud, Google's ad service receives some information from your device, as Google describes in its own disclosures: your approximate location (worked out from your IP address), device identifiers, app and ad interactions (such as app launches, taps and ad views) and diagnostics (crash and performance data), plus basic facts about the device and app. Google uses this to deliver, measure and protect the ads, not to build an advertising profile of you. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Update check

When you open the game, it asks the Google Play Store on your device whether a newer version of Pancake Pile is available, so it can offer you the update. This check is done by Google Play and sends nothing about you to us.

Children

Because the game is for all ages, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The ad settings above are applied to every player.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date, and the game will be updated with it.
