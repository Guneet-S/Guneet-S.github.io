Privacy policy for Pancake Pile

Last updated: 2026-11-24

Pancake Pile ("the game") is a stacking game for Android made and published on Google Play by GPS Agents ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does with your information.

Who we are

Developer: GPS Agents, the developer name shown for the game on Google Play. Privacy contact: ag.gamingstudio2020@gmail.com.

The short version

The game itself collects no personal data and plays offline. It shows small banner ads from Google AdMob. Those ads are child-directed and non-personalised only, and the game does not use the advertising ID.

What the game stores

The game keeps these things on your device only: your best height, your coins, which pancake skins and themes you have unlocked and which one is selected, whether sound and music are switched on or off, and your chosen language. All of it stays on the phone only: it is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob, a service of Google, to show one small banner ad. There are no pop-up ads and no rewarded ads in this version.

  • Every ad request is marked as child-directed and as from a user under the age of consent, so ads are child-directed and non-personalised only. Ads are limited to content rated G (general audiences).
  • No advertising ID is used: the game removes the advertising ID permission.
  • To show and count an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process limited device data: the IP address (and the approximate location worked out from it), the device model, the operating system version, the language, app information such as the app version, device or other IDs (never the advertising ID), the ad views and taps, and diagnostic data of the ads SDK (such as app launch time and hang rate). It is used to show and measure ads, for analytics of the ads and to prevent fraud; with child-directed treatment Google does not use it for personalised ads or remarketing. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Without an internet connection no ad is shown, and the whole game still works.

What the game does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share any personal data of its own.
  • It uses the internet only for the banner ads.
  • It has no in-app purchases, no rewarded ads and no pop-up ads.
  • It uses no analytics, tracking or crash reporting of its own.
  • It needs no account and no sign-in.

Permissions

The game uses internet access, only to load the banner ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK also adds permissions to check the network connection, to keep the device awake briefly and to run short background tasks while it loads and counts ads. None of these asks you anything, and none of them gives the game access to your contacts, photos, location or files.

Your choices

  • To delete everything the game stores on your device, uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings. We keep no copy on any server.
  • Data processed by Google for the ads is handled under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).
  • For any question or request about your data, email us at the address below.

Children

The game is made for players of all ages, including children under 13. Every player is treated as a child for ads, so ads are child-directed and non-personalised only and the advertising ID is not used. The game asks no one for personal information. Game progress stays on the device. The game has no chat and no purchases. The only way out of the game is a tap on a banner ad.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes what it does with data, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
