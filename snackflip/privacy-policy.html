Privacy policy for Snack Flip: Memory Match

Last updated: 2026-11-22

Snack Flip: Memory Match ("the game") is a memory-match game for Android made by GPS Agents. This policy explains what the game does with your information.

The short version

The game itself collects no personal data and works offline. It shows small banner ads from Google AdMob on the menu, the calendar and the Sticker Book. Those ads are child-directed and non-personalised only, and the game does not use the advertising ID.

What the game stores

The game keeps these things on your device only: your best score, whether sound and music are switched on or off, and your Daily Snack Calendar progress (the daily stars, stickers and best times). Daily stars, stickers and best times stay on the phone only: they are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob, a service of Google, to show one small banner ad at the bottom of the menu, the calendar and the Sticker Book. There are never ads on the game board, the pause screen or the result screens.

  • Every ad request is marked as child-directed and as from a user under the age of consent, so ads are child-directed and non-personalised only. Ads are limited to content rated G (general audiences).
  • No advertising ID is used: the game removes the advertising ID permission.
  • To show and count an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process limited device data: the IP address (and the approximate location worked out from it), the device model, the operating system version, the language, app information such as the app version, device or other IDs (never the advertising ID), the ad views and taps, and crash logs and diagnostic data of the ads SDK. It is used to show and measure ads, for analytics of the ads and to prevent fraud; with child-directed treatment Google does not use it for personalised ads or remarketing. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Without an internet connection no ad is shown, and the whole game still works.

What the game does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share any personal data of its own.
  • It uses the internet only for the banner ads.
  • It has no in-app purchases, no rewarded ads and no pop-up ads.
  • It uses no analytics, tracking or crash reporting of its own.
  • It needs no account and no sign-in.

Permissions

The game uses vibration, for a short buzz when you find a pair, and internet access, only to load the banner ads.

Children

The game is made for players of all ages, including children under 13. Every player is treated as a child for ads, so ads are child-directed and non-personalised only and the advertising ID is not used. The game asks no one for personal information. Game progress stays on the device. The game has no chat and no purchases. The only way out of the game is a tap on a banner ad on the menu, calendar or Sticker Book screens.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes what it does with data, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
